## How Should You Assess the Formula?

For a probiotic product, useful questions include:

- Does the label identify the specific probiotic strain?
- Are storage instructions clear?
- Are the quantities relevant to the research cited?
- Does supporting research examine the complete product?

A species name and CFU count are starting points for comparison, rather than a complete assessment.

## Advertised Benefits and Expectations

Neotonics is marketed for digestive balance and skin wellness. That positioning does not establish that it treats a digestive disorder or produces a particular cosmetic result.

Avoid judging effectiveness from selected testimonials alone. Look for information about study design, participant numbers and outcomes that matter to you.
